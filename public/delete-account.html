Delete Your SendMe Account

SendMe Africa ("SendMe") — account & data deletion request

This page lets you request deletion of your SendMe account and personal information without needing the SendMe app installed. It works for any SendMe account — Customer, Runner, Merchant, or Affiliate.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number, password, and any linked Google sign-in are permanently removed from your account.
  • You will no longer be able to sign in with this account, on any SendMe app or portal.
  • If you submitted ID verification as a Runner, that record is deleted.
  • On completed or cancelled orders, tasks, and bookings, the specific pickup/drop-off address, map coordinates, and any notes you entered are removed.
  • On resolved or closed support tickets, the contact email/phone copy stored on the ticket is removed.

What may be retained

As explained in our Privacy Policy, we retain certain information for as long as needed to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. In practice, this means:

  • The basic facts of completed transactions — that an order or task happened, its amount, and its date — stay on record for accounting purposes, no longer tied to your name or contact details, only to your deleted account's internal ID.
  • An order, task, or support ticket that is still active or unresolved when you delete your account is not altered until it reaches a completed/closed state — so an in-progress delivery or an open support case isn't broken by your account being deleted partway through.
  • If you own a merchant storefront with an order still being fulfilled, that storefront's contact details are kept until that order is done — other customers may still be relying on it.

We do not invent or promise a specific retention period here beyond what our Privacy Policy already describes, and we do not claim that every trace of every field is erased everywhere — only that the policy above is what our systems actually do.

If you have an unwithdrawn balance in your SendMe wallet, please withdraw it before requesting deletion — deletion cannot complete while a balance remains, since once your account's contact information is removed we would have no way to pay it out.

Already have the SendMe Customer app installed? You can delete your account directly from Profile → Delete Account — no need to use this page.

Request account deletion

Enter the email address on your SendMe account. If it matches an account, we'll email you a confirmation link — clicking it is what actually deletes your account, so no one else can delete it using only your email address.

Confirm account deletion

You followed a deletion link we emailed you. This step is what actually deletes your account and personal information — it cannot be undone.

Prefer email, or having trouble with this page? Contact us directly at support@sendmeafrica.com and we will process your deletion request manually.
