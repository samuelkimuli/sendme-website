This Privacy Policy explains how SendMe Africa ("SendMe," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the SendMe customer app, website, runner app, or merchant portal (together, the "Services"). SendMe operates in Uganda. By using the Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account and authentication information

When you create a SendMe account, we collect your full name, phone number and/or email address, and a password (stored only as a one-way hash — we never store or can retrieve your plaintext password). If you sign in with Google, we receive your verified name and email from Google to create or match your account. We use this information to authenticate you and issue a secure session token each time you sign in.

Location and address data

When you place an order, book a service, or request an errand, we collect the pickup and drop-off coordinates and address text you provide or confirm for that specific request, using Google Maps to display and help you select locations. We do not maintain a saved address book, and we do not track your location in the background — location is only read at the moment you create or interact with a request.

Shopping (marketplace) data

When you place an order through SendMe's marketplace, we collect the items ordered, quantities, prices, and your delivery address so the order can be fulfilled.

Service bookings and task ("errand") requests

When you request a service or errand, we collect the category, description, pickup/drop-off details, your budget, urgency, and preferred schedule, and the payment method and status for that request.

Payment information

SendMe currently processes payments through mobile money (for example, MTN Mobile Money). To process a mobile money payment, we collect the phone number you use to pay. SendMe does not collect or store card numbers or other card data — we do not currently support card payments.

Voice input

If you use the voice-input option when describing a service request ("Ask SendMe"), we record short audio clips and send them securely to our AI service request assistant (powered by Google's Gemini model) to interpret your request. If this feature is unavailable, the app falls back to manual text entry. You can always type your request instead of using voice.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and authenticate your sign-ins.
  • To process and fulfil your orders, bookings, and errand requests.
  • To process payments you initiate.
  • To connect you with a Runner or Merchant for your request.
  • To communicate with you about your orders, requests, and account.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Services, including detecting fraud and abuse.
  • To improve and support the Services.

3. Sharing With Runners and Merchants

SendMe shares only the information necessary to complete your request. When a Runner is assigned to your task, they see your name and the relevant pickup/drop-off addresses — not your phone number or payment details. When you order from a Merchant, the Merchant sees your name and delivery address — not your phone number. Communication between you and your assigned Runner or Merchant happens through in-app messaging rather than by sharing direct contact numbers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. If you would like us to delete your account and associated personal information, see Your Rights and Choices below.

6. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and storing passwords only as irreversible one-way hashes. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or ask us to stop processing it, by contacting us at support@sendmeafrica.com. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable time. Account and data deletion is currently handled by our support team on request rather than as a self-service in-app action.

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. International Data Processing

Some third-party providers listed above (such as Google and Cloudinary) may process data on servers located outside Uganda as part of providing their services to us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above when we do, and material changes will be communicated through the app or website.

This policy describes SendMe's actual data practices as implemented in the SendMe apps today. It will be updated as those practices change.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at support@sendmeafrica.com.
